⚖️ Legal Rights AI Assistant

📌 About the Project

Legal Rights AI Assistant is an AI-powered application designed to help users understand general legal rights and access legal information in a simple and easy-to-understand way.

The project aims to make basic legal information more accessible through an interactive AI-powered assistant.

🚀 Live Demo

"Open Legal Rights AI Assistant" (https://legal-rights-ai-assi-jvyi.bolt.host)

✨ Features

- ⚖️ Legal rights information
- 🤖 AI-powered assistance
- 💬 Interactive question and answer support
- 📚 Easy-to-understand legal information
- 🔎 Quick access to general legal guidance
- 📱 Simple and user-friendly interface
- 🌐 Accessible online

🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is to help users learn about their general legal rights and understand legal information more easily through an AI-powered platform.
